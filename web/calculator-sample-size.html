When NOT to use this

  • Means only: it plans t tests on roughly normal data with equal group sizes. Pass/fail proportions, unequal groups, non-parametric, equivalence and non-inferiority tests are outside this version.
  • It does not plan designed experiments (factorials, blocking, replication); that needs a different tool.
  • The standard deviation is a planning guess. A pilot-sample SD tends to understate the real one, so look at the sensitivity rows.
  • Power answers “will I detect a real difference of this size”, not “is that difference important”.
  • The cost of missing a real effect and the chance it is real are judgments. Treat the cost-minimizing n as a range: the table shows how flat the cost is near it.
  • Not a substitute for a statistician when the design is complicated (clustered, repeated or sequential data).
Methods: sample-size formulae and the t-iteration per NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook §7.2.2.2; exact power from the noncentral t distribution, validated against the Minitab two-sample example and SciPy reference values. Educational tool, not professional advice. Inputs are processed locally in your browser and are never transmitted.
