When NOT to use this

  • Two options only. There is no multi-supplier comparison.
  • Costs are linear in volume: no volume discounts, price breaks or step-fixed costs (such as a second machine at higher volume). Above the capacity of a single machine, this model understates the cost of making it yourself.
  • Volume is flat: no growth or seasonality. Pre-tax, single currency.
  • Intellectual property, core competence and supplier dependence are not priced. The risk premium is a blunt stand-in.
  • Defect costs, carrying rate, risk premium and inventory days are assumptions. The sensitivity grid shows how much they matter.
