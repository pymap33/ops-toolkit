When NOT to use this

  • It does not measure defects. The reduction percentages are your assumptions, not predictions.
  • No "hidden factory" multiplier is applied. Enter hidden costs as your own line items only if you can defend them.
  • Lost customers and brand damage are not estimated unless you enter them as a line.
  • Savings are assumed flat, arriving at the end of each year. There is no ramp-up period.
  • Illustrative business-case tool only, not for regulated cost reporting.
Framework: prevention–appraisal–failure (PAF) classification per ASQ. Educational tool, not professional advice. Inputs are processed locally in your browser and are never transmitted.
