When NOT to use this

  • Demand over the lead time should be roughly normal and the lead time constant. Lumpy, intermittent or heavily skewed demand, and variable lead times, are outside this version.
  • It models a continuous-review (Q, R) policy only, not periodic review or min/max systems. Shortages are assumed backordered, not lost.
  • No quantity discounts or seasonality. Holding cost is a single number you supply.
  • The cost per unit short is the weakest input. The full cost table shows the range where the answer barely changes.
  • It is not a forecast. A poor estimate of demand variability gives a poor safety stock.
  • Fill rate and cycle service level are different measures; the table shows both.
Methods: EOQ and safety stock per Fundamentals of Operations Management, §8.5 and §8.7; the optimal service level is a standard critical-ratio result validated independently. Educational tool, not professional advice. Inputs are processed locally in your browser and are never transmitted.
